## Finding 1 — Toss KR daily = KRX + NXT combined from NXT launch
- 005930 / 000660, unadjusted Toss vs pykrx (KRX): 40/40 days identical up to 2025-02-28; from
  2025-03-24 (NXT coverage of these symbols) open/high/low/close often differ and Toss volume is
  1.3-1.9x KRX. Toss open = NXT 08:00 pre-market, close = NXT 20:00 after-market.
- The rules fill at the KRX regular session, so open fills and stops after 2025-03 were wrong.
- Fix: `data/krx_overlay.py` — for KR symbols with Toss bars from 2025-03-04, KRX adjusted bars
  (pykrx) are stored in data/kr/krx/ and replace the Toss rows from 2025-03-04 at load time
  (`store.load_bars`, used by `load_panel`). Toss files stay untouched (incremental collect works).
- Scale check before use: median |krx/toss close - 1| <= 0.5% on shared dates. Measured on 20
  symbols: 0-0.02% (ETFs ~0.01% rounding); single days up to ~9% are NXT after-hours moves.
  Mismatch → no overlay, Toss kept, counted in data/kr/krx_overlay.json and report limitations.

## Finding 2 — Toss returns suspended days as volume 0 with frozen prices
- e.g. 000040 56 rows, 0000Y0 198 rows with volume 0 and O/H/L/C = previous close.
- Old `clean_daily` dropped only volume 0 AND a zero price (pykrx style), so frozen Toss rows stayed
  and a backtest could buy/sell at a pre-suspension price (optimistic, e.g. exiting before a
  delisting-bound halt). Fix: drop every volume-0 row (no trade, no fill possible) → NaN → held through.
- The trial files were collected with the old rule; they are deleted and re-collected in the full run.
